FPL Gameweek 2 Predicted Lineups: Latest Starting XI Predictions

by Wooi Kin Khor
Aug 28, 2026, 6:41 AM UTC
Getty Images

With Matchday 1 now behind us, there is already more information available to help predict how each Premier League side will set up this weekend.

These predicted line-ups consider Matchday 1 selections and performances, each manager’s preferred system, player fitness and injuries, suspensions, and the latest transfer activity. The predictions should become increasingly accurate as the season progresses and managers establish their preferred starting XIs.

Fantasy Football Scout notes that its predictions are fine-tuned closer to the deadline and following the pre-match press conferences.

Crystal Palace vs Manchester City

Crystal Palace predicted line-up (3-4-2-1):
Henderson; Tomiyasu, Richards, Canvot; Muñoz, Kamada, Wharton, Mitchell; McNeil, Pino; Mateta.

Manchester City predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Donnarumma; Khusanov, Rúben Dias, Gvardiol, O’Reilly; Anderson, Marc Guehi; Foden, Cherki, Semenyo; Haaland.

Liverpool vs Nottingham Forest

Liverpool predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Alisson; Frimpong, Jacquet, Van Dijk, Kerkez; Szoboszlai, Gravenberch; Víctor Muñoz, Wirtz, Gakpo; Isak.

Nottingham Forest predicted line-up (3-4-2-1):
Sels; Diomande, Milenkovic, Murillo; Aina, McAtee, Sangaré, Williams; Ndoye, Gibbs-White; Igor Jesus.

Coventry City vs Hull City

Coventry City predicted line-up (4-3-3):
Rushworth; Van Ewijk, Thomas, Amenda, Jay da Silva; Yirenkyi, Grimes, Onyeka; Tchaouna, Awoniyi, Mason-Clark.

Hull City predicted line-up (4-3-3):
Tzolakis; Ajayi, Egan, Mendy, Coyle; Crooks, Slater, Giles; Belloumi, McBurnie, Stroud.

Bournemouth vs Everton

Bournemouth predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Petrovic; Smith, Hill, António Silva, Truffert; Cook, Scott; Rayan, Kluivert, Tavernier; Evanilson.

Everton predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Pickford; Röhl, Tarkowski, Branthwaite, Mykolenko; Armstrong, Hackney; Ndiaye, Dewsbury-Hall, George; Barry.

Tottenham Hotspur vs Newcastle United

Tottenham Hotspur predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Kinsky; Porro, Van Hecke, Senesi, Robertson; Tonali, Mateus Fernandes; Moore, Gallagher, Tel; Solanke.

Newcastle United predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Hornícek; Dedic, Thiaw, Botman, Lewis Hall; Steur, Miley; Elanga, Jacob Ramsey, Barnes; Wissa.

Chelsea vs Brighton

Chelsea predicted line-up (3-4-2-1):
Sanchez; Fofana, Lacroix, Colwill; Gusto, James, Moises Caicedo, Hato; Palmer, João Pedro; Rogers.

Brighton predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Verbruggen; Wieffer, Vuskovic, Boscagli, Kadioglu; Ayari, Groß; Gómez, Hinshelwood, De Cuyper; Kostoulas.

Leeds United vs Brentford

Leeds United predicted line-up (3-4-2-1):
Trafford; Rodon, Bijol, Muharemovic; Bogle, Ampadu, Stach, Justin; Wilson, Aaronson; Calvert-Lewin.

Brentford predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Kelleher; Kayode, Ajer, Collins, Lewis-Potter; Janelt, Sangaré; Dango Ouattara, Jensen, Schade; Thiago.

Sunderland vs Fulham

Sunderland predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Roefs; Meunier, Ballard, Alderete, Reinildo; Xhaka, Sadiki; Hume, Le Fée, Angulo; Brobbey.

Fulham predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Leno; Castagne, Andersen, Bassey, Robinson; Berge, Iwobi; Bobb, King, Palacios; Gonzalo García.

Manchester United vs Ipswich Town

Manchester United predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Lammens; Dalot, Maguire, Heaven, Shaw; Tielemans, Andrey Santos; Mbeumo, Bruno Fernandes, Dorgu; Matheus Cunha.

Ipswich Town predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Scherpen; O’Shea, Diop, Greaves, Davis; Lukic, Núñez; Fatawu, Enciso, Maeda; Emersonn.

Aston Villa vs Arsenal

Aston Villa predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Suzuki; Wan-Bissaka, Lindelof, Torres, Maatsen; McGinn, Kamara; Cash, Barkley, Garnacho; Buendía.

Arsenal predicted line-up (4-2-3-1):
Raya; White, Mosquera, Gabriel, Calafiori; Rice, Lewis-Skelly; Saka, Odegaard, Tzolis; Havertz.
